2. WHAT IS GEMINI CODER (ANTIGRAVITY)?
--------------------------------------------------
Gemini Coder (Antigravity) is an agentic AI coding environment developed by Google DeepMind. Instead of just answering text prompts in a browser, it works directly inside your local development workspace as an active pair programmer:
- It understands full codebase architectures, scripts, and local media.
- It executes terminal builds, renders Remotion video compositions locally, and manages assets.
- It operates using specialized "Skills" (like this B-roll engine) triggered with simple slash commands (e.g. /broll).
- It guides you step-by-step through interactive click-and-choose question modals, so you never have to guess or write complex prompts.

--------------------------------------------------
3. WHAT DOES THE B-ROLL SKILL DO?
--------------------------------------------------
This skill transforms YouTube script sentences into broadcast-grade, high-retention editorial B-rolls (in the visual language of Vox, Johnny Harris, Bloomberg, MKBHD, and Cleo Abram):
- 8 Modular Spec Archetypes: Hardware specs, scoreboards, split versus comparisons, live telemetry gauges, Reddit/forum receipts, statement hooks, comma lists, and forensic audits.
- Synchronous Acoustic Foley: 11 frame-accurate sound effects (swoosh, click, pop, thud, stamp_slam) matched to visual cues.
- Material-First Intelligence: Proactively searches for and downloads authentic transparent product cutouts and 4K footage instead of drawing cheap synthetic shapes.
- Interactive Onboarding: Type /broll, and it interviews you with one simple click-and-choose question at a time to build your scene.

5. QUICK 2-MINUTE SETUP
--------------------------------------------------
1. Download or clone the repository, then open the folder in Antigravity IDE (the skill in .agents/skills/broll/ is detected automatically).
2. Open the terminal and run:
   npm install
3. In the Antigravity chat panel, type:
   /broll

Antigravity will guide you through creating your first clip!
