### Legend of Archetype Specs:
- **Spec 1 (`ProductSpec`)**: Hardware teardown, specs, leader lines, exploded CAD
- **Spec 2 (`RatingScore`)**: Broadcaster scoreboard, weighted category marks, review badges
- **Spec 3 (`Comparison`)**: Conceptual splits, head-to-head versus, OTA vs Recall
- **Spec 4 (`MetricTelemetry`)**: Decibel needles, thermal gauges, live price waveforms
- **Spec 5 (`QuoteReceipt`)**: Community forum receipts, Reddit cards, highlighter draws
- **Spec 6 (`StatementSpec`)**: Provocative thesis hooks, lone words, giant stat punches
- **Spec 7 (`SequentialEmphasis`)**: Comma-separated list items, progressive locks
- **Spec 8 (`ForensicAudit`)**: Dense multi-pillar audit rows, mechanical clicks, verdict stamp
